# AdFection: When Digital Advertising Becomes a Tool for Spying

**An educational article exploring how digital advertising infrastructure can be exploited to deliver spyware and target devices without user interaction.**

This repository contains the Arabic and English versions of an article examining **AdFection**, the abuse of digital advertising technology, and the privacy and security risks associated with online advertising infrastructure.

## About the Article

Digital advertising relies on a complex ecosystem of platforms, exchanges, identifiers, and user data. While these technologies are designed to connect advertisers with audiences, the same infrastructure can potentially be abused for malicious purposes.

This article explores how **Discover-Tech** allegedly exploited digital advertising infrastructure in a technique documented by **Citizen Lab** as AdFection.

It covers:

- **Digital Advertising Infrastructure:** An introduction to the advertising ecosystem and Real-Time Bidding (RTB).
- **Discover-Tech:** Background on the company, its related platforms, and its advertising technology.
- **The AdFection Technique:** How targeted advertisements can act as a delivery mechanism for spyware without requiring users to click on them.
- **The Al Jazeera Case:** Citizen Lab's findings concerning targeted devices and spyware infections.
- **Who Is at Risk?:** Potential risks to journalists, human rights defenders, lawyers, and ordinary internet users.
- **Privacy Protection:** Practical steps to reduce tracking exposure and improve device security.

## Key Takeaway

Online privacy is not only about what we share intentionally. Data collected through everyday digital services can reveal information about our interests, behavior, and devices.

Understanding how advertising technology works, how it can be abused, and what protections are available is an important step toward a safer digital environment.
